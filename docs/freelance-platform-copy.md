## Upwork: focused profile

**Title:** AI Automation Engineer | RAG, Document Workflows & Safe Agent Systems

**Overview:**
I help teams turn repetitive, document-heavy work into reliable AI-assisted workflows. I start by understanding the process, data, review requirements, and success criteria—then build a focused solution your team can actually operate.

Typical projects include document extraction and validation, internal knowledge assistants, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and agent-assisted operational workflows. My approach prioritizes observability, evaluation, guardrails, and human review where it matters.

If you have a workflow that is slow, repetitive, or hard to scale, send a short description of the current process, the tools involved, and the outcome you want. I will tell you whether an AI workflow audit is a sensible first step.

**Specialized profile:** RAG & AI knowledge systems — semantic search, source-grounded answers, retrieval evaluation, permissions-aware workflows, and practical handover documentation.

## Fiverr gigs

### I will audit your business workflow for AI automation opportunities

**Deliverables:** workflow map, automation opportunities, data and risk review, prioritized roadmap, 30-minute walkthrough.

**Scope:** one workflow and its existing tools. **Exclusions:** custom software development, production deployment, or legal/compliance certification.

### I will build a source-grounded RAG assistant for your internal knowledge

**Deliverables:** scoped RAG workflow, source-aware answer behavior, basic evaluation set, setup documentation, handover demo.

**Scope:** agreed document set and one user workflow. **Exclusions:** unrestricted autonomous actions, proprietary model training, and ongoing hosting costs.

### I will build an AI document-processing workflow with human review

**Deliverables:** extraction/validation pipeline, exception and review path, test examples, deployment notes.

**Scope:** one document type and agreed output fields. **Exclusions:** guarantees of perfect extraction or unattended high-impact decisions.

## Client questions

1. What is the current workflow, and who performs it?
2. What outcome would make this project successful?
3. Which tools, documents, systems, or data sources are involved?
4. What mistakes would be costly or unacceptable?
5. What needs human approval before an action is taken?
6. Do you have sample data that can be safely shared after an NDA if needed?
7. What timeline and budget range are you considering?
